About Silent SOS

📱 How It Works

Silent SOS is an emergency alert system disguised as a calculator app. In dangerous situations, you can discreetly trigger an emergency alert that sends your location and medical information to pre-configured contacts.

🚨 Emergency Triggers

📢 How to activate emergency:
🧮 Type "100=" on calculator
🔴 Click SOS button
👆👆👆 Triple tap screen
📱 Press volume button 3x
📳 Shake phone vigorously

🔒 Privacy & Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No data is shared without your explicit trigger
  • Location is only sent during emergencies
  • Medical info is encrypted in transmission

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Modern web browser with geolocation support
  • Internet connection for alerts
  • Location services enabled
  • Modern smartphone (iOS 12+ / Android 8+)

📞 Emergency Services

  • 🚓 Police: 100
  • 🚑 Ambulance: 102
  • 👩‍⚖️ Women Helpline: 1091
  • 🚒 Fire: 101
